📊 Season Awards Philosophy

Every season's reward breakdown — including the split between championship payouts and season awards — is voted on and agreed upon by the full league before the season begins. No commissioner unilaterally decides the money.

Season 1 is being assembled now. The final payout structure will be voted on by all confirmed managers before Opening Day. The current percentages shown are the starting proposal.

Why We Prioritize Season Awards

Even if you're out of playoff contention, there's real money on the line. Batting title, ERA leader, most walk-offs, first no-hitter — every game you play has stat progression and cash value. We intentionally keep the awards pool meaningful so that finishing the season is always worth your time.
